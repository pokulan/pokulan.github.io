Privacy Policy - The Hobo

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Hobo ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you install and play it. The App is developed and published by an independent developer (contact details at the end of this policy), not by Google or any other third party.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information we collect ourselves

The optional friends features (adding friends, sending in-game packages, comparing scores with friends) work only when you are signed in to Google Play Games. They send limited data to our own server, including:

  • Your Google Play Games player ID and in-game nickname (your Play Games display name)
  • Friend invites you send and accept (the IDs and nicknames of the players involved)
  • Packages you send to friends: the item, the amount, and the short message (up to 60 characters) you write
  • Your best scores, which are shown to your friends

Pending invites and packages are deleted from the server once the receiving player opens them in the game. This data is used only to operate these features and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

1.2 Information collected by third-party services we use

The App integrates the following Google services, each of which may collect data under its own privacy policy (linked below). We do not control what these services collect; we only receive the results they provide back to the App (e.g. "is this player signed in", "did this ad finish playing").

  • Google AdMob (advertising) - shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to support free play. The App does not request the Advertising ID, so ads are served on a non-personalized/contextual basis rather than based on a profile of you. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google Play Games Services - used for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save (so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch devices). This stores your game save data in your Google account's app data, tied to your Google Play Games profile. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing - processes in-app purchases (removing ads, buying in-game coins). Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment card information, only confirmation that a purchase was completed.

1.3 Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved locally on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, this local data may be included in your device's backup to your Google account, under Google's policies.

1.4 Device permissions

The App requests only the following permissions:

  • Internet / network state - required for ads, Play Games, Play Billing, and the optional friends features described above.
  • Billing - required for in-app purchases through Google Play.

The App does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to: operate and maintain the App's features (saves, friends features, purchases); display and measure advertising; diagnose and fix bugs; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising and Your Choices

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The App does not collect your device's Advertising ID, so ads you see are not based on a personal profile built from your activity across apps. You can remove ads entirely with the "remove ads" in-app purchase.

You can also use your device's own ad settings (e.g. Android Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or limit ad personalization at the operating-system level.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App contains mature themes (such as alcohol, drugs, and crude humor) and is intended for adult players. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.

5. Data Retention

We retain the friends-feature data described in section 1.1 (your nickname, player ID, and scores) for as long as you use these features, or until you request deletion. Invites and packages are removed once they are delivered. Data held by Google (Play Games saves, billing records, ad measurement) is retained and deleted according to Google's own policies, linked above.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights for data we hold ourselves (section 1.1), contact us using the details below and include your in-game player ID (shown in the friends screen). For data held by Google (Play Games, Billing, AdMob), use Google's own account and privacy controls, or contact Google directly.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle. Please note that the friends features currently communicate with our server over an unencrypted (HTTP) connection, so avoid putting anything personal or sensitive in package messages. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
