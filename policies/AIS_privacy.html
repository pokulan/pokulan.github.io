This Privacy Policy describes how Alive in Shelter ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you install and play it. The App is developed and published by an independent developer (contact details at the end of this policy), not by Google, MobiBurn, Pollfish, or any other third party.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information we collect ourselves

Some social features (friend requests, in-game chat, comparing stats with other players) send limited data to our own server, including:

  • Your in-game nickname and a player ID generated for your account
  • In-game actions you take that affect other players (e.g. friend requests, challenge invites, chat messages)
  • Basic gameplay statistics shown to friends (e.g. coins, win/loss counts, days survived)

This data is used only to operate these social features and is not sold or shared with advertisers.

1.2 Information collected by third-party services we use

The App integrates the following Google services, each of which may collect data under its own privacy policy (linked below). We do not control what these services collect; we only receive the results they provide back to the App (e.g. "is this player signed in", "did this ad finish playing").

  • Google AdMob (advertising) - shows banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads to support free play. The App does not request the Advertising ID, so ads are served on a non-personalized/contextual basis rather than based on a profile of you. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) - shows a consent form to players in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland as required by applicable law, and remembers your choice. You can change your choice at any time from the in-game Settings screen ("Privacy options" / GDPR button).
  • Google Play Games Services - used for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save (so your progress can be restored if you reinstall the App or switch devices). This stores your game save data in your Google account's app data, tied to your Google Play Games profile. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing - processes in-app purchases (e.g. removing ads, buying in-game currency). Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; we never receive your payment card information, only confirmation that a purchase was completed.

1.3 Device permissions

The App may request the following device permissions, used only for the stated purpose:

  • Photos/Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES on Android 13+, READ/WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older versions) - only to save an in-game screenshot you choose to take to your device's gallery.
  • Internet / network state - required for ads, Play Games, Play Billing, and the optional online social features described above.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to: operate and maintain the App's features (saves, social features, purchases); display and measure advertising; diagnose and fix bugs; and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell personal information.

3. Advertising and Your Choices

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The App does not collect your device's Advertising ID, so ads you see are not based on a personal profile built from your activity across apps. If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent message on first launch; you can revisit and change that choice at any time from the in-game Settings screen.

You can also use your device's own ad settings (e.g. Android Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or limit ad personalization at the operating-system level.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App's core content is rated for a general audience; some optional content is age-gated and intended only for adult players who confirm their age within the App. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can remove it.

5. Data Retention

We retain the social-feature data described in section 1.1 for as long as your account/profile is active, or until you request deletion. Data held by Google (Play Games saves, billing records, ad measurement) is retained and deleted according to Google's own policies, linked above.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights for data we hold ourselves (section 1.1), contact us using the details below. For data held by Google (Play Games, Billing, AdMob), use Google's own account and privacy controls, or contact Google directly.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we handle, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
